Reels are rounded up, assuming two 2.4 m lengths can be cut from each reel. Totals are rounded to cents; per-gate figures use unrounded totals.

The €6 is an illustrative allowance, not a verified customs bill. The EU's temporary €3 duty for qualifying low-value imports depends on the goods and import arrangements; the actual charge and any additional fee are unconfirmed. Check the checkout total and avoid counting fees twice. - **Tubes:** each gate needs four 600 mm frame tubes and four legs of about 300 mm (30 mm in the bracket, about 250 mm free, 20 mm in the end cap). The saw cut comes off the leg, so it ends up about 297 mm long; the exact leg length does not matter. Prices are DOLD cart prices for 50 or more pieces of one length, including cutting and logistics.
- **LEDs:** 4 × 0.6 m = 2.4 m (144 LEDs) per gate, so one 5 m reel covers two gates. €11.70 per reel was observed by the project owner on 27 October 2026 for the linked ECO/IP30 variant. Variant price, VAT, availability for ten reels, order charges and shipping could not be independently verified. A provisional €6 order-charge allowance is included; separate LED shipping is excluded. Datasheet: [Worldsemi WS2812B](https://www.world-semi.com/products/ws2812b-v6.html).
- **Filament:** 4 corners, 2 foot brackets, 4 end caps and 12 diffuser segments add up to about 545 cm³ of solid part volume per gate, roughly 0.7 kg of PETG. Infill lowers the real amount somewhat. The price assumes €10–15 per kg.
- **Small parts:** short wires to solder the strip across each corner, plus heat shrink and solder. Rough estimate.
- **Not included:** controller, power supply, cables between gates, and the optional M3 screws and inserts.
